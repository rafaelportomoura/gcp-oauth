# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-23_

This policy applies to OAuth applications associated with this repository.

## Information we access

When you sign in with Google, the app may access basic account information made available by Google OAuth scopes, such as:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your profile identifier

## How information is used

This information is used only to authenticate you and operate the app functionality.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is not shared with third parties except as required to operate the app or comply with legal obligations.

## Data retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for app operation and security.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in this repository.
